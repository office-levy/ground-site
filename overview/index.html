Visual research, gathered in one place on your Mac.

What Compile does

  • Put pictures, PDFs, links and videos on boards, and arrange them in rows, columns and grids.
  • Find pictures by what they show, or more like one you have.
  • Ask about a board’s pictures and PDFs, and have it line up, group and sort them for you.
  • Share a board with people you invite, work on it together, or publish it to the web.
  • Your library stays in a folder on your Mac.

Signing in with Google

If you sign in with Google, Compile asks Google for a private ID for you and your email address, and nothing else: not your name, contacts, mail or files. The ID signs you in. The address tells Compile whose account it is, and recognises someone it invited; other people on Compile never see it. Privacy Policy
